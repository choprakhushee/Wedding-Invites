01 — The Darbar

आमंत्रण

An invitation is the first scene of a family’s story.

Aamantran is Hindi for invitation. An atelier is the couturier’s workshop. Together they promise an invitation made by hand, with the patience of couture.

Our look is the Palace Parlour — an old-world Rajasthani parlour, patterned and collected, in maroon, bottle green, blush and gold. Your invitation is always your own.

05 — The Atelier

Not a template shop

Every piece is art-directed. We compete on taste and craft, and we give each family the time that takes.

  1. I

    Conversation

    We listen to your families, your functions and the feeling you want your guests to have.

  2. II

    Sketch

    Motifs, palette and lettering, drawn by hand around your story.

  3. III

    Storyboard

    Every scene and every reveal, timed to the music before a frame moves.

  4. IV

    Motion

    Slow, deliberate animation, reviewed on your own phone first.

  5. V

    The reveal

    One link for every family group — and a first impression worth remembering.
